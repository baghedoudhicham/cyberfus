Trust & Security

Our commitment to security, compliance, and transparency.

Compliance

Cyberfus is SOC 2 Type II certified and undergoes regular third-party penetration testing. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of security and data protection for our customers.

Open Source

We believe in the power of open source. Our agent and rule sets are open source and available on GitHub. We welcome contributions from the community.

Audits

Our platform is audited annually by independent third-party auditors. Reports are available to enterprise customers upon request.

Encryption

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption. We utilize TLS 1.2+ for all communications.
